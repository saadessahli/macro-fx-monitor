No audio is bundled with Macro FX Monitor.

Add only royalty-free, generated, or personally owned music to this folder.
For example, add `subtle-macro-bed.mp3` and set:

  "musicEnabled": true,
  "musicUrl": "/audio/subtle-macro-bed.mp3",
  "musicVolume": 0.08

Leave `musicEnabled` false or `musicUrl` empty to render silently.
